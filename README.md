# Cadence

A to-do list you would actually come back to.

Get it on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cadence.todo).

## What is in it

Tasks sit under Today, This Week or This Month. Each one can have a priority, a time
estimate, a list like Work or Shopping, notes, a checklist of steps, a reminder, and a
repeat. You can type a task or say it.

A ring shows how much of today is done, next to a daily streak and a level that
fills as you finish things.

Any task can open into a focus timer of 15, 25, 45 or 60 minutes, with rain, ocean
or deep background sound on Pro.

The stats page shows your current and best streak, how many tasks you have done in
total, and the hours of the day you tend to finish the most.

There are about twenty themes, from Sakura and Matcha through Nord and OLED
black, with Mocha, Rose and Neon Night among the Pro ones.
